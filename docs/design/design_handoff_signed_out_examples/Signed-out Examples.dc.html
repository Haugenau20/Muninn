1Signed-out example data · NPCs and Locations · dark theme

What I think is wrong today: the example shows 3 people and 2 places, with no status bar, no search, no filters and no location groups. A visitor sees a sparse, unfinished-looking list, while the real directory (your 22 NPCs) is a busy, grouped, filterable record. The example undersells the product.

What changes: the Ashen Vale setting grows to 10 NPCs and 10 locations. Every row uses the real cells (NPCRowCells / LocationRowCells), tokens come from colour-schema.json (dark), and there's still no invented author anywhere. That's why the expanded row in 1d leaves out "Recorded by". Member-only controls (Add NPC, Select NPCs) are left out.

Options: 1a/1e are today's pages for comparison. 1b/1f are the full page, inert, under the panel. 1c/1g put the example beside the panel so it's above the fold. 1d/1h are a live sample with a slim sign-in banner, and their filters, bands, search and row expand all work.
